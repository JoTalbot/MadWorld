# MadWorld — Content Plan

## Content model

All gameplay content should be data-driven.

Primary entities:

- Item
- Blueprint
- VehicleChassis
- VehicleModule
- Facility
- Region
- Settlement
- Faction
- MissionTemplate
- ContractTemplate
- ResourceNode
- WorldEvent

### Regions

Initial world should contain at least five distinct regional economies:

- Dustbelt: scrap-rich, fuel-poor
- Iron Grave: metal-rich industrial ruins
- Salt Flats: open high-speed traversal, scarce water
- Green Ruins: food/water pockets, dense hazards
- Black Ridge: rare materials, extreme danger

### Factions

- Settlement Coalition: stability and trade
- Road Wardens: convoy protection and law
- Iron Dogs: industrial control
- Dust Reavers: raiders and smuggling
- Ash Covenant: mysterious high-risk faction

## Mission families

### Economic

- deliver cargo
- procure material
- manufacture item
- repair fleet asset
- recover stranded vehicle

### Exploration

- scout location
- map route
- investigate signal
- recover lost cache

### Combat

- escort convoy
- intercept raiders
- defend settlement
- eliminate hostile patrol

### Political

- deliver diplomatic package
- increase faction standing
- establish supply link
- enforce blockade objective

## Dynamic world events

Examples:

- refinery breakdown creates sudden fuel demand
- convoy disaster creates salvage opportunity
- faction offensive closes a road
- rain exposes a hidden resource site
- dust storm changes route risk
- settlement water shortage increases water prices
- rare wreck field appears temporarily

## Content quality rule

A new item should normally participate in at least one production chain, trade loop, mission, vehicle fit, facility requirement or strategic objective. Content that exists only as collectible wallpaper has low priority.
